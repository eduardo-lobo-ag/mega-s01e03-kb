How each report was placed

  1. Drop duplicate rows. The export comes in overlapping pages, so one id can appear twice.
  2. Every report takes its tenant’s cause from causes.ts: hint when it came through a booking-v3 form, parser otherwise. This is how the desk’s own cause table counts.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) is also timezone. Its day and month are not swapped, so the parser cannot explain it. It stays in its tenant’s count too.

The intake tag is not used. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the cause depends on the tenant.

Why they ended up together

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). All three problems produce subjects like “Wrong day on the invite”, so subject matching put them in one ticket. The existing cause table shows the hint row with no ticket of its own and leaves the one-day moves in a footnote under parser.
